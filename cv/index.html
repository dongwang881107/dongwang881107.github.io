Research interests

I develop mathematical models and computational methods for medical imaging, with an emphasis on inverse problems, variational regularization and machine learning.

  • Image reconstruction for MRI, CT and nuclear medicine, including PET and SPECT.
  • Electrical impedance tomography and conductivity reconstruction.
  • Scientific computing, parallel programming and research software for medical imaging.
